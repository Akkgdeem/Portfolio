This portfolio has two parts:

Part 1: Environment Setup
Installed and configured a professional development environment using
Cursor IDE with AI extensions (Claude Code and Codex), connected to
a public GitHub repository.

Part 2: Research Project
A deep research project on AI-Powered SEO Content Production,
collecting content from 10 top industry practitioners to build
a foundation for a real SEO playbook.

I chose this topic because it sits at the intersection of two major
shifts happening right now: the rise of LLM-driven search (ChatGPT,
Perplexity, Google AI Mode) and the need for content teams to scale
without sacrificing quality. The goal was to find genuine practitioners
— not theorists — and collect their highest-signal content.

Key Insights from Research:
The biggest shift in 2026
Search is no longer just shifting to AI — it is being re-monetized.
Classic organic click share dropped from 73% to 50% in one year
for top queries (US data). Paid results now capture 36% of clicks,
up from 16%. The old SEO playbook is breaking.

What replaces it"
- From rankings and clicks → visibility, mentions, and citations in LLMs
- From keyword-first → intent, entity, and topical authority models
- From volume → depth, structure, and genuine expertise signals
- From traffic as a KPI → brand visibility and assisted conversions

What works for AI content production
- Build a complete topical map before writing anything
- Optimize content in "chunks" — each section must stand alone
- Use topical clusters: pillar page + cluster pages for full coverage
- AI crawlers cannot process JavaScript — site structure matters
- Boolean questions at H3/H4 level improve AI Overview inclusion
- 95% of ChatGPT users also use Google — optimize for both
