Post 1 — Google Ads Explained Simply
URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dee-m-883367108_ecommerce-digitalmarketing-googleads-share-7454767252538785792-lBPW
Tags: #Ecommerce #DigitalMarketing #GoogleAds #ROAS #PPC

Imagine you're running a small coffee corner on the highway.
You want more people to stop and buy your coffee. So, you ask
a friend to stand nearby holding a big sign: "COFFEE – $1"

That sign? It's your Google Ads.
What's written on it? That's your keywords.

- People driving past and seeing the sign → Viewers = Impressions
- People stop to check it out → Interest = Clicks
- People actually buy a cup → Buyers = Conversions
- How much you make for every $1 spent on that friend → ROAS

It all comes down to:
Getting attention → Sparking interest → Driving action → Generating revenue

Simple in theory. Powerful in practice. 😉

Post 2 — Product Content Before Ads
URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dee-m-883367108_ecommerce-growth-amazonfba-share-7453132860934328321-C1CK
Tags: #Ecommerce #Growth #AmazonFBA #CRO #ProductOptimization

High-performing ads don't start with campaigns.
They start with product content.

If your product page isn't clear, no ads will fix it.

I worked on a before & after optimization for Amazon and other
platform products: titles, bullet points, description, images,
videos, and A+ content. No extra traffic. No major changes. Just:

- Showing the problem → solution clearly
- Highlighting the main benefits visually
- Making the content easier to read and understand

The result:
- Higher conversion rate
- Better buyability
- Faster customer decisions
- 1,000–2,000 daily visitors
- The brand became searchable on Google

Because when people understand the product quickly, they buy.
And that's the same logic behind great ads:
Clear message → Strong visuals → Relevant to the customer

Simple always wins. 😄
