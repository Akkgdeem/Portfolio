Video SEO Experiments: AI Content vs Human Content
Topic:What controlled testing reveals about AI content performance

Key points:

- PageOptimizer Pro is built on the principle that SEO should be tested scientifically, not guessed at
- Has run multiple experiments comparing AI-generated vs human-written content in identical on-page conditions
- Key finding: Google does not penalize AI content by default it penalizes thin, unhelpful content regardless of source
- On-page optimization signals (heading structure, keyword placement, semantic coverage) remain strong ranking factors in 2026
- The sites that got hit by spam updates were not penalized for using AI they were penalized for publishing at volume without quality controls

Specific on-page tactics that still work in 2026:
1. Include boolean questions (yes/no answerable) in H3/H4 headings
2. Answer questions by repeating the question at the start of the answer
3. Keep direct answers to ~320 characters for featured snippet eligibility
4. Use facts and numbers remove opinion and filler from direct answers
5. Use synonyms and related terms avoid exact keyword repetition
6. Cross-link between semantically related pages consistently

Bottom line:
AI content production works when paired with proper on-page structure.
The structure is what PageOptimizer Pro tests and the results
are replicable across industries and niches.

- Recovery from an AI content penalty is extremely slow prevention is the only real strategy
- Surviving sites had genuine topical authority built over time
